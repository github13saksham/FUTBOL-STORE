# Shipping Policy

We provide shipping across all major cities and regions in India.

## Processing Time
- Orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days.
- Customized orders may require additional processing time.

## Order Confirmation
- Once your order is successfully placed, you will receive a confirmation email/message.
- A separate notification will be sent once your order is packed and shipped.

## Delivery Time
- Standard delivery generally takes 5–10 business days, depending on your location.

## Tracking Information
- Tracking details will be shared through email/message once the order has been shipped.
