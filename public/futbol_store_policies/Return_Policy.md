# Refund, Return, and Cancellation Policy

Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong product, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with clear photos/videos at thefutbolstore.in@gmail.com.

## Returns & Exchanges
- Returns are accepted only for damaged or incorrect items.
- Customized jerseys (name/number printing) are non-returnable unless damaged or incorrect.
- Items must be unused and in original condition.

## Refunds
- Once approved, refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable.

## Cancellations
- Orders cannot be cancelled once they have been accepted and processed.
- Customized jerseys cannot be cancelled once customization or production has started.
- Cancellation requests are accepted only before the order processing stage begins.
- In case of duplicate or accidental orders, please contact us immediately for assistance.

For any refund, return, or cancellation queries, contact us at thefutbolstore.in@gmail.com.
